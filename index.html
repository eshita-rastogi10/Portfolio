UK EV Charging Infrastructure Investment Case

End-to-end investment case for a 500-charger UK EV programme - Excel financial model, sensitivity analysis, and McKinsey-style pitch deck built for infrastructure IC submission.

Policy Advisory  ·  Development Finance

Local Currency Financing in Emerging Market Infrastructure

World Bank / McKinsey-style policy memo on currency mismatch as the structural failure point of emerging market infrastructure finance - with case studies and DFI recommendations.

  • Documents the 60% hard-currency / 5% USD-revenue mismatch in Sub-Saharan Africa - a 15% depreciation on $200m adds ~$30m to repayment, equivalent to 2-3 years of EBITDA
  • Three case studies (Indonesia PT SMI, Kenya NSE bonds, Philippines peso PPA) establish replicable local currency structures that do not require sovereign guarantees
  • Prescriptive DFI recommendations: 20% local currency portfolio floor, $5bn hedging vehicle recapitalisation, NDC pipeline alignment - structured by actor and horizon

Financial Modelling  ·  Power Generation

3-Statement Financial Model - 500MW PPA Power Co

Interview-grade, fully-linked 3-statement model for a 500MW PPA-backed power generation company - balance sheet balances to zero across all five projection years.

  • Revenue built from first principles: Capacity (MW) × CF% × 8,760hrs × PPA Tariff ($/MWh) - PPA escalator drives top-line; D&A and interest are the two compression levers
  • Working capital mechanics via DSO/DPO/Inventory Days - AR, AP, and inventory changes flow through indirect-method CFO, keeping all three statements mechanically linked
  • Credit metrics include DSCR (CFADS basis), Net Debt/EBITDA, interest coverage, and ROE - DSCR rising from 1.01x to 1.42x as $700m debt amortises over the model period

Data Analysis  ·  Policy Strategy

UK EV Adoption vs Charging Infrastructure Gap - Data Analysis

Data-driven strategy analysis quantifying the UK's structural EV infrastructure deficit across 12 regions and four 2030 investment scenarios - built entirely in Excel.

  • EVs-per-charger ratio doubled from ~13 to ~26 between 2018-2024 - infrastructure falling further behind at a 44% EV CAGR vs 27% charger CAGR; gap reaches ~40x by 2027 on current trajectory
  • North East England, Wales, and Northern Ireland rated CRITICAL - 30%+ below national average charger density; market-led deployment alone will entrench regional inequality
  • ZEV mandate scenario requires 16× current installation run rate (8,200 → 130,000 chargers/yr) and ~£19.5bn over the decade; gap analysis built across four scenarios from Conservative to ZEV Mandate
